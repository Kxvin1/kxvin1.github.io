Needs you badges

Plan reviewa plan waits for your review: approve it, send it back, pause it or queue it
Decidea choice only you can make: say go or not, or pick one of its options
Approvebuilt work, or work ready to start, waits for your approve or your go
Readsomething to read, with nothing to decide
Your tasksomething only you can do yourself: a sign-in, a run, opening a session
Answer to your questionClaude's answer to a question you asked: read it, then close it

Waiting for badges

test lockwaits for the shared test lock: another stage's full gate or ship holds it
a workerwaits for a worker session to finish its part or answer
a shipwaits for another stage to ship its files live
a free slotwaits for a worker slot to free up (5 at most at once)
a runwaits for a run to finish: a test run or another job it started
MAINwaits for MAIN, the session that runs the others, to answer or act
a stagewaits for another stage (a copy of the tools being built) to finish
Claude's holdClaude held it on purpose until what its why names happens, then starts it again

What a hold waits for

a datewaits for a date it names, such as a check that needs a week of real use first
an eventwaits for something to happen on its own that it must watch, such as the next real run or promotion
other work firstwaits for the work ahead of it to finish first, in the order you set
your askwaits for your answer to one of the asks under Needs you
your wordwaits until you say to start it again

Testingits change is in the full test run going now
Holding the test locka long job of its own (a recording, a run started by hand) holds the shared test lock; changes wait behind it
Waiting for CPUits test is held because the machine is busy; it starts by itself
In linequeued for the shared test run; the number is its place
Waitingits worker is open but waits before its next step; the words after it say on what
